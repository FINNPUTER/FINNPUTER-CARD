Waitlist rules

Short and plain. This is how the FINNPUTER Card waitlist works.

Your number

When you join you get a number. Numbers are handed out in the order people join. Lower numbers go first.

Your number is your place in line, so it can change. It gets lower when you invite friends or when someone ahead of you leaves. It gets higher when someone behind you invites friends and moves past you.

What a low number gets you

  • Cards go out in number order, as each country opens.
  • The first metal batch is limited. It goes out in number order too.
  • The waitlist gets pricing by email before it is published on this site.

Inviting friends

Everyone on the list has a personal invite link. Every friend who joins with your link moves you up 10 places, or to the front if fewer than 10 people are ahead of you.

A friend counts once their signup is complete. If we ask new members to confirm their email address, the friend counts when they have confirmed it.

Every invite stays linked to you. What inviters get on top is announced together with pricing. Until then no reward is owed.

Fair play

One person, one entry. Entries made with invented or throwaway addresses do not count. Inviting your own second address does not count either. We can remove such entries and undo the places they moved.

What the waitlist is not

A number is a place in line. It is not a card, not a contract and not a promise that the card will be offered in your country. Whether and when you can order also depends on where you live and on the identity check that is part of the card programme.

Leaving

Every email we send has a link to remove your address. You can also write to finnputer@proton.me. When you leave, your number and your answers are deleted. See the privacy page for what we store.

Changes

We may change these rules while the card is being built. If a change affects your number or your invites, we tell you by email first.
