What we collect

  • Your email address and country of residence, when you join the waitlist.
  • Your answers to the optional questions: which card you want, how you would fund it, how much you expect to spend, what price you find fair, whether you use FINNPUTER and whether you hold $FINNPUTER.
  • The date you joined and, if present, the site or campaign that sent you here.
  • Your waitlist number and your personal invite code. If you joined through a friend's invite link, we store which link brought you. If friends join through yours, we store that they came through you, so that invites can be credited to you later.
  • If you join through the FINNPUTER Trade bot: your Telegram user ID, whether you were a Premium member and whether you held the minimum $FINNPUTER amount in your bot wallet at that moment, and the Telegram user ID of the member who referred you in the bot, if there is one. We use this to give you and them the extra places described in the waitlist rules. We do not receive your wallet address, your balance or your trades.
  • Proof of your consent: the time you ticked the box, the wording you agreed to, your IP address and your browser type. If you confirm your address through an email link, we store the time and IP address of that confirmation as well. If your email address is corrected after you joined, we keep the address entered first next to this proof, and how often the confirmation email was sent again.

We do not ask for your name, your wallet address, identity documents or payment details on this site.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies and uses no advertising or analytics tools from third parties. To count a visit only once, your browser keeps a small marker that is deleted when you close the tab. If you arrive through an invite link, your browser remembers its code until you join. If you arrive through the FINNPUTER Trade bot, your browser remembers the signed link from the bot until you join. After you join, your browser remembers your own invite code so this page can show your place in line when you come back. You can clear both at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser. Fonts and images are served from this site, not from third parties.

Why we use it

We use your email address to tell you about the FINNPUTER Card, for example when early access opens. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You gave it by ticking the box on the form. We keep the proof of consent because the law requires us to be able to show that you agreed (Art. 7(1) GDPR).

We add up the answers of everyone on the list, for example signups per country and expected monthly spending, and share these totals with card providers to negotiate the card programme. We do not share your email address or your individual answers with them.

Who processes the data for us

  • Railway hosts the waitlist server and its database.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. Like any web host it processes your IP address to deliver the page.
  • If we send you a confirmation email, an email delivery provider processes your email address for that purpose.

These providers may process data in the United States. Transfers rely on the safeguards these providers offer under EU law, such as standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

We keep your waitlist entry until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it, and at the latest until the waitlist is closed.

Your rights

You can withdraw your consent at any time. You can also ask us what we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, restrict its use, or receive a copy. Write to finnputer@proton.me from the address you signed up with. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority, for example the CNPD in Portugal.
